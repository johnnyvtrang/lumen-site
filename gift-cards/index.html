How it works

Buy it online. They book when it suits them.

  1. 01

    Buy the card

    Pick an amount on the gift-card page (opens in a new tab). Add a note if you like. Payment goes through Square.

  2. 02

    It's sent by email or text

    Square sends the card to the email address or phone number you enter.

  3. 03

    They book

    They text me or use the quote builder, and mention the gift card. Mobile at their home, or drop-off.

  4. 04

    The card pays

    The card is used at payment, in person or on the Square invoice. If the service costs more, they pay the rest. If it costs less, the balance stays on the card.

Questions

Gift card questions.

Do Lumen gift cards expire?

No. They're Square eGift cards, and Square gift cards have no expiration date.

How is the gift card delivered?

By email or text. When you buy it, you enter the email address or phone number it goes to. You can send it to yourself and pass it along.

How much should I put on it?

Any amount works. For a sedan, $99 covers an Exterior Refresh and $149 a Full Refresh. Larger vehicles cost more, and the quote builder prices any vehicle. If the service costs more than the card, they pay the rest at the visit.

What can the gift card be used for?

Any service I offer: detailing, headlight restoration, paint correction, coatings and powersports.

How do they use it?

They book by text or through the quote builder and mention the gift card. It's used at payment, in person or on the Square invoice. Any balance left over stays on the card.
