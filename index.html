02 · What I see most

What a year here does to a car.

Sun, hard water, salt and daily life. Most of it comes off, and the walkaround tells us how much.

  • Swirls & haze

    Fine scratches dull the paint. Lights show what daylight hides.

  • Road salt

    Salt and packed mud build up underneath all winter.

  • Example

    Dull headlights

    Lenses yellow and haze over with sun and age.

  • Pet hair & kid mess

    Pet hair, car seats and crumbs work into seams and carpet.

Stock photos, not Lumen jobs. The headlight is toned to show an aged lens.

03 · Method · The Lumen Standard

Nothing guessed, nothing rushed.

A repeatable process, the way a lab runs a protocol.

  1. 01

    Inspect & measure

    We look at the car together first, under proper light. I photograph its condition before I touch it, and paint depth is measured before any polishing.

    • Paint depth · µm, every panel
    • Condition photos
  2. 02

    Decontaminate

    pH-neutral snow foam and iron remover, plus tar and mineral removal, before anything touches the paint.

    • pH-neutral foam
    • Iron · tar · mineral
  3. 03

    Correct

    Gauged panel by panel, before and during polishing, so defects are leveled while keeping as much clear coat as possible.

    • Gauged before & during
    • Clear coat kept
  4. 04

    Protect

    Protection is matched to your finish: wax soap, ceramic soap, ceramic sealant, or a coating-safe topper.

    • Matched to finish
    • Maker-rated durability
  5. 05

    Document

    A last pass under detailing lights, then before & after photos sent to you, so you can see exactly what changed.

    • Inspected under lights
    • Before / after photos
