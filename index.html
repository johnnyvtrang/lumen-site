Signature service · Headlights

Headlight restoration, done to last.

Store kits take off some haze, but without a multi-stage machine polish the lens is left finely scratched, so it clouds again sooner. At Salt Lake's elevation the sun is stronger than at sea level, so factory lens coatings tend to break down sooner here. I machine-correct every lens through three polishing stages, seal it with a ceramic UV coating, and back it with a 1-year clarity warranty. A new headlight assembly often costs several hundred dollars per side, and $1,000 or more on many luxury cars.

Utah extras

Utah winters & trail days

Undercarriage flushes

Utah roads get salt and magnesium chloride all winter, and it sits where you can't see it — frame rails, brake lines, suspension. A normal wash never reaches it.

What you get with either flush

  • Degreaser worked into the frame rails, crossmembers, suspension, brake-line runs and wheel wells
  • A thorough rinse that flushes salt, magnesium chloride, mud and road grime out of the seams underneath
  • Leaves some anti-corrosion protection on the metal underneath, to help slow rust

Protect

Ceramic coating by me, in your garage — request a quote below. PPF, wraps, tint and chrome deletes through my install partner.

Ceramic soap, ceramic spray, ceramic coating — three different things. Every Clean & Protect detail finishes with a ceramic soap (Gtechniq W3 Ceramic GWash): a slick, water-beading layer its maker rates for up to 3 months. Clean, Restore & Protect adds a hand-applied ceramic spray (Adam's graphene ceramic spray), panel by panel, that its maker rates for 12–18 months. Both are light, renewable layers you top up at your next detail. A ceramic coating is a different product: a much harder, semi-permanent layer that bonds to the paint and lasts years — and because it locks in whatever is underneath, the paint is polished first. I apply paint coatings in your garage; glass, wheel and tire coatings can be added to any visit.

FAQ

Good questions.

The short answers on visits, weather, timing and payment. Don't see yours? Text me.

Do I need to be home?

No. For mobile jobs, leave the key in a lockbox or another secure spot we agree on when you book, and make sure I can reach water and power. Please take valuables out or lock them away. I'll text when I arrive and when I'm done, with photos.

Do you bring your own water and power?

Not yet. Mobile appointments need an outdoor spigot and an outlet within about 50 ft. Headlight-only jobs need just an outlet. If you don't have both within reach, text me before booking and I'll tell you what can work.

What if the weather turns?

I watch the forecast. If rain, snow or freezing temperatures would hurt the result, I'll text you the evening before. You can move the appointment to the next open day. Weather changes never cost you a fee. Headlight-only jobs can often still go ahead if the car is in a garage or carport. From about November to March, mobile visits cover interiors, rinseless washes, headlights and undercarriage flushes; full exterior details resume in April.

How long will it take?

Your quote shows the time for your exact vehicle. On a sedan, a Clean takes about 1 hour inside or out, and a Full Clean, Restore & Protect takes about 6 hours. Bigger vehicles take longer. Jobs over about 7.5 hours are split over two visits on back-to-back days. Paint correction and ceramic coatings are booked as their own full day or two.

How do I pay? Is there a deposit?

Yes — a deposit of 25% of your quote, rounded to the nearest $5 ($25 minimum), holds your date, and it comes off your final bill. Once we've picked a time, I'll text you a secure payment link to hold your date. Cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours ahead and your deposit moves to the new date or is refunded; later cancellations and no-shows keep it. If I call off a visit for weather, your deposit always carries over. The rest is due when the work is done — I take major credit and debit cards. Fleet accounts are invoiced monthly, with no deposit. Prices are before Utah sales tax where it applies.

Will you try to upsell me?

No. You get your price up front, itemized for your vehicle, before you book. At the walkaround, if something on your quote isn't needed — or is only partly needed — I take it off or discount it. If I find something you didn't mention, I show you and tell you the price, and nothing is added unless you say yes.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell me within 48 hours and I'll come back and make it right.

Why not just use a headlight kit from the store?

Kits take off some haze, but without a multi-stage machine polish the lens is left finely scratched, so it clouds again sooner. I machine-correct the lens through three polishing stages, apply a ceramic UV coating, and back it with a 1-year clarity warranty — if it yellows or clouds within a year, I re-polish and re-coat it free.

Why wash under the car?

Two reasons in Utah: winter salt and trail mud. Road salt and magnesium chloride collect on the frame, brake lines and suspension, where a normal wash never reaches. A Winter Salt Flush (from $45) is a thorough degreasing wash of everything underneath that lifts the salt and grime off and leaves some anti-corrosion protection behind — a cheap way to slow rust. After Moab or the dunes, the Trail Flush (from $65) is the same degreasing wash, built to clear packed mud from skid plates, suspension and wheel wells before it dries hard and traps moisture (mud packed solid +$50). Both are available year-round — whenever you want one.

Can you remove water spots from sprinklers or hard water?

Usually, yes. Light spots sitting on the surface come off chemically (from $45). Spots baked on by the sun need a stronger mineral remover and clay (from $95). If rings are still there after that, the minerals have etched into the glass or clear coat and need machine polishing — I'll show you a test spot and quote it before going further. Afterward I seal the surface so spots don't bond as easily next time.

Ceramic soap, ceramic spray, ceramic coating — what's the difference?

They're three different products. Ceramic soap (every Clean & Protect detail; I use Gtechniq W3 Ceramic GWash) is a ceramic-infused wash that leaves a slick, water-beading layer for up to 3 months (the maker's rating). Ceramic spray (Clean, Restore & Protect; Adam's graphene ceramic spray) is hand-applied panel by panel after the paint is decontaminated and is rated by its maker for 12–18 months. Both are light layers that are easy to renew at your next detail. A ceramic coating (Gtechniq) is a much harder, semi-permanent layer that bonds to the surface and lasts years. Because it locks in whatever is underneath, the paint is polished first. Paint coatings are available now if you have a garage I can work in (request a quote); glass, wheel and tire coatings can be added to any visit. Your quote always says which one you're getting.

Can you do paint correction and ceramic coating at my place?

Yes. Paint correction — Gloss Enhancement or the 2-step — is done on-site, outside or in your garage; you don't need a garage for it. Ceramic paint coatings and wheels-off wheel coatings need a garage I can work in, so the coating goes on and cures out of the sun, wind and dust, and the paint is always polished first. Glass, wheel-face and tire coatings can be added to any visit. No garage? Drop-off is coming once I have a workspace.

My car has a ceramic coating from another shop. Can you maintain it?

Yes. Refresh Washes and Decon & Reseal are built for coated cars. Tell me who installed it — some coating warranties require an annual inspection by the original installer.

Is it safe for my matte paint or wrap?

That's the specialty. Matte-specific products only, no wax or gloss enhancers, and careful technique at every film edge.

My car has Alcantara, chrome, carbon or a soft top. Is that extra?

Only when there's enough of it to need its own products and time. These are never part of a level — they cost the same whichever level you choose, and your quote shows the price for your vehicle. Large Alcantara or microsuede areas (seats and door inserts; an Alcantara headliner is part of Headliner cleaning) are from $30: a dedicated cleaner, almost no moisture, no steam or heat, and the nap brushed back up by hand. Chrome window trim, emblems and handles are from $59, polished by hand and sealed; a lot of chrome — grille, bumpers, mirror caps, running boards — is from $99, with the trim included. A convertible soft top is from $70: fabric is cleaned and reproofed, vinyl is cleaned and UV-protected. Exposed carbon fiber is from $49, cleaned and sealed with no polish or abrasives. Small pieces — a shift boot, steering-wheel trim, a carbon mirror cap, a few chrome emblems — are included at no charge. Genuine suede, nubuck, aniline leather and classic single-stage paint are quoted after a test spot, because water can mark those leathers and old paint can react to polish.

Can you clean my kid's car seat?

Yes. One child car seat is included with every Interior or Full Clean & Protect, and two with Clean, Restore & Protect; any others (or any at Clean) are $35 each. Crumbs and spills are vacuumed out, the shell and buckles are cleaned, and the fabric cover is cleaned following the seat manufacturer's instructions. Harness straps are surface-cleaned with mild soap and water only, never steamed or soaked, because that can weaken the webbing. I'll take the seat out to clean underneath it, but for safety please reinstall it yourself (or have a certified car seat technician check it).
