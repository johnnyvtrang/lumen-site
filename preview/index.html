04 · Chemistry

Science behind the shine.

Four reasons the method matters, measured in what happens to your paint and lenses.

pH 7

Neutral by design

pH-neutral soaps lift dirt without stripping the protection or coating already on your paint.

Fe

Iron, made visible

Iron remover turns purple as it dissolves embedded brake dust, so you can watch it work before it rinses away.

Ca2+

Salt Lake hard water

Minerals in hard water leave spots that can etch clear coat if they’re left to bake in the sun.

UV

Why headlights yellow

Sunlight breaks down the lens’s factory coating. Restoration removes the failed layer and recoats it.
