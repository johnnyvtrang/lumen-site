Questions

Before you book.

Raised on engines, driven by science: I’m happy to explain exactly what goes on your car and why.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave the key in a lockbox or another secure spot we agree on when you book, and make sure I can reach water and power. Please take valuables out or lock them away. I’ll text when I arrive and when I’m done, with photos.

How long will it take?

Your quote shows the time for your exact vehicle. On a sedan, a Full Clean takes about 2 hours and a Full Clean, Restore & Protect about 6. Bigger vehicles take longer, and jobs over about 7.5 hours are split over two visits on back-to-back days.

Ceramic soap, ceramic spray, ceramic coating: what’s the difference?

Three different products. Ceramic soap (Clean & Protect; Gtechniq) leaves a slick, water-beading layer for up to 3 months. Ceramic spray (Clean, Restore & Protect; Adam’s graphene ceramic spray) is hand-applied panel by panel after the paint is decontaminated, and is rated by its maker for 12–18 months.

Both are light layers that are easy to renew at your next detail. A ceramic coating is a much harder, multi-year layer, applied in your garage over freshly polished paint. About ceramic coatings.
