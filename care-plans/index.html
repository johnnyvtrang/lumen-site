Plan visits are billed after each visit; only your first plan visit needs the booking deposit. Plans start with a Full Radiance, a Protected Full Detail, or an inspection visit. The plan price assumes at least 3 visits on 2-week and monthly plans and 2 on 2-month and quarterly plans. Stop any time — no cancellation fee. If you stop before your third visit (second on 2-month and quarterly plans), the visits you've had are billed at the regular one-off price, with the difference added to your last visit. The member 10% applies to add-ons and other services; where a bundle or multi-vehicle discount already applies, you get whichever discount is larger. Prices before Utah sales tax where it applies.
