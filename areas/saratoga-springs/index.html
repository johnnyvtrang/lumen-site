Local notes

Detailing in Saratoga Springs.

What I see most on cars here, and what I'd do about it.

  1. 01

    Boats and lake days

    Lake water leaves water spots and a waterline on hulls. Boats are priced per foot and done where they're parked; please confirm your storage lot or HOA allows washing.

  2. 02

    HOA rules

    If you have an HOA, check its rules on driveway washing. Interiors and headlight restoration don't involve a wash at all.

  3. 03

    Hard-water spots

    The city's pressurized irrigation water isn't drinking water and can carry elevated dissolved solids, so sprinkler spots on glass and paint can be stubborn. A glass coating helps them wipe off instead of bonding, once the glass is prepped.
