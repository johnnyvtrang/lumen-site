Local notes

Detailing in Cottonwood Heights.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    Class Three storm days

    UDOT's 2025 rule added a Class Three level for major storms in both Cottonwood canyons, with a minimum of 5/32 inch of tread, and the Cottonwood Heights Police Department is one of two city departments that enforce it.1 A Class Three day is a storm day on a canyon road, and whatever that road carried settles into the frame rails and wheel wells. We flush it out on the first day back above freezing, over a containment mat, with the run-off collected and disposed of properly.

    Winter Salt & Trail Flushes

  2. 02

    A day parked at the resort

    UDOT can now enforce the traction rules up to 24 hours ahead of a storm, and it points out that a storm can roll in while you're parked up the canyon.1 A car that sat through a snowfall at the base area comes home with slush packed around the wheels and grime on any roof box or hitch rack. Renew includes one extra you can spend on that gear, cleaned and UV-protected in place; Radiance includes two.

    Mobile detailing

  3. 03

    Canyon-stream water, harder in summer

    Salt Lake City supplies parts of Cottonwood Heights, and more than 60 percent of its water starts in canyon streams; in summer it adds deep wells, which it describes as harder.2 In its 2025 figures the Big Cottonwood plant measured 136 mg/L total hardness, about 8 grains per gallon, while wells and springs reached 571.3 If you're on that water, summer spots can be heavier than winter ones, so we test one on the glass before choosing a remover (from $50).

Questions

Cottonwood Heights questions.

Is there a travel fee in Cottonwood Heights?

No. Cottonwood Heights sits between Holladay and Sandy, inside our core valley zone.

Do you need my driveway for a flush?

Only enough flat space for the containment mat to sit under the car. A level stretch of driveway or the garage floor both work; a steep drive doesn't.

Can you clean a ski box or bike rack that stays on the car?

Yes. It can be the extra Renew includes, or one of two at Radiance: cleaned and UV-protected in place. A faded box can also be restored on its own.
