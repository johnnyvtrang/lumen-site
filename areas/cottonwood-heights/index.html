Local notes

Detailing in Cottonwood Heights.

What I see most on cars here, and what I'd do about it.

  1. 01

    Canyon trips and road salt

    Utah's roads are salted all winter, and the brine collects on frame rails, brake lines and suspension. A Winter Salt Flush degreases everything underneath and leaves some anti-corrosion protection on the metal.

  2. 02

    Ski boxes, racks and roof tents

    Renew includes one extra you can spend on gear and Radiance two, cleaned and UV-protected in place. A faded roof box can be restored on its own.

  3. 03

    Strong sun

    UV gets stronger with altitude, and the whole valley sits above 4,000 feet. Headlights and black trim fade in the sun; restoration plus a UV coating slows it down, and the headlights carry a 1-year clarity warranty (3 years with Headlight restoration, Grade III, a sprayed 2K clear coat).
