Local notes

Detailing in Salt Lake City.

What I see most on cars here, and what I'd do about it.

  1. 01

    Condos and apartments

    If you park in a shared garage or an assigned stall, interior-only details, headlight restoration and rinseless winter washes can often be done right there, as long as I have an outlet within about 50 feet and your building allows it. Exterior washes need a spigot and somewhere for the runoff.

  2. 02

    Inversion season and road film

    Winter inversions hold pollution near the valley floor, and salty road spray adds to it, so glass and paint pick up a dull film. Iron and tar removal in Reflect (Clean & Protect) lifts it, and the windshield is cleaned inside and out.

  3. 03

    Faded headlights and trim

    On older cars, faded headlights and chalky black trim are usually the first signs of age. Both are fixed, not covered: the lens is machine-corrected and recoated, the trim gets a ceramic trim coat.
