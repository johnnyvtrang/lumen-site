Recent work.

Real Lumen jobs in the Salt Lake Valley, each written up the way we record it: what we found, what we did and why, how long it took, and how to look after it. Shown with each owner's OK. Where a job has no photos yet, it says so rather than borrowing someone else's.

  1. Ceramic coating · Mazda CX-5

    A white CX-5, coated for easy cleaning

    Heavy sprinkler water spots taken off least aggressive first, polishing only where they had etched, then chrome, emblems, Alcantara seat inserts, the steering wheel, piano-black trim and one layer of ceramic coating.

    Time
    5–6 h
    Layers
    1

    Yellowed CR-V headlights, clear again

    Both lenses yellowed and hazed, a safety concern for the owner. Sanded, polished and finished with a ceramic coating.

    2.5 h
    Grade
    II
    Clarity warranty
    12 mo

    A white ML550, inside and out

    Vape smell and oil spills inside, scratched door-handle cups, dull black wheels. Steam, hand polishing and a ceramic layer on the wheels and tires.

    Time
    8 h
    Level
    Renew, full

    Grade II
