Recent work · Ceramic coating

A nearly new GR86, coated nose to diffuser.

By Johnny Trang, founder · November 2026 · Toyota GR86 · Salt Lake Valley

A white GR86 with a few thousand miles got a one-step polish on its lower panels, then one ceramic coating across the paint, wheel faces, glass, trim, exhaust tips and carbon-fiber parts. Window tint was installed by our install partner.

Time on the car
6–7 h
Correction
one-step, lower panels
Coating layers
1
Maker rating
up to 5 yr

What we found

A nearly new car with a few thousand miles on it. The owner wanted to save on polishing, so we polished only the lower part of the car and coated the rest as it was.

What we did and why

  1. Wash and decontamination. Even a new car carries bonded iron and road film that a wash can't reach, and a coating bonds only to clean paint.
  2. One-step polish, lower portion only. The lower panels are where road grit and wash marks build up first. Everything above was inspected and coated as it was.
  3. Panel wipe, gloves on from here, so polish oils don't stop the coating bonding.
  4. Coating, one layer, everywhere it belongs: paint, wheel faces, glass, trim, exhaust tips, the carbon-fiber lip, diffuser and side skirts. One coating across the exterior means water and grime release the same way everywhere, and the trim and carbon parts get UV protection too.
  5. Cure, then inspection under detailing lights.

Window tint was installed by our film install partner, not by us.

Coating on this job

A Gtechniq ceramic coating, one layer. Gtechniq rates it for "up to five years of protection when properly maintained".

What it took

About 6–7 hours on the car for our part (the tint is separate). One layer of ceramic coating, maker-rated up to 5 years when properly maintained.

Honest limits: a coating adds gloss, water behavior and easier washing, but it doesn't stop rock chips, and a sports car's front end is where chips land first. The upper panels weren't polished, so the coating seals them as they were. Photos of this car aren't processed yet; they'll be added here when they are.

Care

  • Indoors and dry for 12 hours after application, per the maker.
  • pH-neutral soap, two buckets, dry after washing. No wax, no automatic brush washes.
  • Tint: follow the install partner's own care instructions.

The full routine is in How to wash a ceramic-coated car, and how we match a coating to a car is in How to choose a ceramic coating.
