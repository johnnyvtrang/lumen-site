02

Protect

Ceramic coating by me, in your garage. You choose the coating separately from the correction grade: the grade fixes the paint, the coating protects it. PPF, wraps, tint and chrome deletes through my install partner.

Ceramic soap, ceramic spray, Radiance+, ceramic coating — four steps up. Every Reflect (Clean & Protect) detail finishes with a ceramic soap (Gtechniq W3 Ceramic GWash) that its maker rates for up to 3 months. Radiance (Clean, Restore & Protect) adds a basic hand-applied ceramic spray — Gtechniq C2 (rated by its maker for up to 6 months) or Cerakote Platinum Rapid Ceramic (rated for 50 washes). Radiance+ upgrades any detail to a spray coating: Gyeon Q² CanCoat EVO (up to 12 months / 12,000 km) or Adam's Graphene Ceramic Spray Coating Advanced (rated 12–18 months), from $129 on a sedan — Radiance+ on the detailing page. A ceramic coating is a different product: a much harder, semi-permanent layer that bonds to the paint and lasts years — and because it locks in whatever is underneath, the paint is polished first. I apply paint coatings in your garage; glass, wheel and tire coatings can be added to any visit.

Measured, not guessed

Clear coat is finite.

Factory clear coat is typically about 35–50 microns, thinner than a sheet of paper, and every pass of a polisher takes a little of it. So every panel is gauged before I polish and again as I go, a test spot comes first, and I take only as much as the finish needs.

You get 50/50 photos, half corrected and half not, so you can see what changed.

Questions

Coatings & correction.

Can you do paint correction and ceramic coating at my place?

Yes. Paint correction — any of the three grades — is done on-site, outside or in your garage; you don't need a garage for it. Ceramic paint coatings and wheels-off wheel coatings (quoted in person) need a garage I can work in, so the coating goes on and cures out of the sun, wind and dust, and the paint is always polished first. Glass, wheel-face and tire coatings can be added to any visit. No garage? Drop-off is coming once I have a workspace.

Ceramic soap, ceramic spray, ceramic coating — what's the difference?

They're different products, each a step up. Ceramic soap (every Reflect (Clean & Protect) detail; I use Gtechniq W3 Ceramic GWash) is a ceramic-infused wash that leaves a slick, water-beading layer for up to 3 months (the maker's rating). Ceramic spray (Radiance (Clean, Restore & Protect)) is a basic hand-applied spray after the paint is decontaminated: Gtechniq C2, rated by its maker for up to 6 months, or Cerakote Platinum Rapid Ceramic, rated for 50 washes. Radiance+ upgrades any detail to a spray coating — Gyeon Q² CanCoat EVO (up to 12 months / 12,000 km) or Adam's Graphene Ceramic Spray Coating Advanced (rated 12–18 months). All three are light layers that are easy to renew at your next detail. A ceramic coating (Gyeon, Gtechniq or Cerakote; you choose) is a much harder, semi-permanent layer that bonds to the surface and lasts years. Because it locks in whatever is underneath, the paint is polished first. Paint coatings are available now if you have a garage I can work in (find your coating); glass, wheel and tire coatings can be added to any visit. Your quote always says which one you're getting.

My car has a ceramic coating from another shop. Can you maintain it?

Yes. Refresh Washes and Decon & Reseal are built for coated cars. Tell me who installed it — some coating warranties require an annual inspection by the original installer.

Is it safe for my matte paint or wrap?

That's the specialty. Matte-specific products only, no wax or gloss enhancers, and careful technique at every film edge.
