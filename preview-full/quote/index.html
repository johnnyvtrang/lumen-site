How booking works

Clear terms, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Deposit

    25% of your quote, rounded to the nearest $5 ($25 minimum), holds your date and comes off your final bill. Fleet accounts are invoiced monthly, with no deposit.

  2. 02

    Changes & cancellations

    Cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours ahead and your deposit moves to the new date or is refunded. Later cancellations and no-shows keep it.

  3. 03

    Weather

    If rain, snow or freezing temperatures would hurt the result, I'll text you the evening before. Weather changes never cost you a fee, and your deposit always carries over. From November to March, mobile visits cover interiors, rinseless washes, headlights and undercarriage flushes.

  4. 04

    Payment

    The rest is due when the work is done. I take major credit and debit cards. Prices are before Utah sales tax where it applies.

  5. 05

    On-site

    Mobile appointments need an outdoor spigot and an outlet within about 50 feet, and room to work around the car. Headlight-only jobs need just an outlet. Ceramic paint coatings and wheels-off wheel coatings need a garage I can work in. Prefer drop-off? You bring the vehicle to me, with no travel fee; the drop-off location is shared when you book.

  6. 06

    Made right

    Not happy? Tell me within 48 hours and I'll come back and make it right.
