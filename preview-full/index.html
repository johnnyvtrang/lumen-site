Every surface has its own chemistry. We match the care to each one.

04 · Method · The Lumen Standard

Nothing guessed, nothing rushed.

Measure first. Remove only what's needed. Protect what's left.

  1. 01

    Inspect & measure

    We look at the car together first, under proper light. We photograph its condition before we touch it, and paint depth is measured before any polishing.

    • Paint depth · µm, every panel
    • Condition photos
  2. 02

    Decontaminate

    pH-neutral snow foam and iron remover, plus tar and mineral removal, before anything touches the paint.

    • pH-neutral foam
    • Iron · tar · mineral
  3. 03

    Correct

    Gauged panel by panel, before and during polishing, so defects are leveled while keeping as much clear coat as possible.

    • Gauged before & during
    • Clear coat kept
  4. 04

    Protect

    Protection is matched to your finish: wax soap, ceramic soap, ceramic sealant, or a coating-safe topper.

    • Matched to finish
    • Maker-rated durability
  5. 05

    Document

    A last pass under detailing lights, then before & after photos sent to you, so you can see exactly what changed.

    • Inspected under lights
    • Before / after photos

07 · The founder

Raised on engines. Driven by science.

Lumen's founder, Johnny Trang, grew up in a family of mechanics and car people, then built a career as a scientist, where you don't trust a claim until you understand the chemistry behind it. He started Lumen to detail cars the same way.

That became the Lumen standard, the same for every car: paint depth gauged before any polishing, anything uncertain tested on a spot first, and every job documented with before & after photos and a care card.
