Lab Notes · Choosing a coating

How to choose a ceramic coating for how you drive.

By Johnny Trang, owner · Updated November 2026 · 5 min read

Pick the coating for the job the vehicle does, not for the brand on the bottle. A trail or hunting rig needs chemical resistance and a surface mud lets go of; a family car needs slick, easy washing; a show car needs gloss, which mostly comes from correcting the paint before the coating goes on.

What actually differs between coatings

Most ceramic coatings cure into a thin, glass-like silica layer on top of the clear coat. Water beads on it, and dirt has less to grip.1 Past that shared chemistry, coatings differ in a few ways that matter to you:

  • Layers. Some are one layer. Others are a base layer for strength and chemical resistance with a slick top layer for beading and dirt release.
  • What they're tuned for. Some lean toward gloss and depth. Others lean toward slickness and easy washing, or toward toughness.
  • Rated life. Each maker rates its own coating, from about 2 years up to about 5 years, properly applied and maintained.2 Real life depends on how the car is driven, washed and stored.
  • The finish they're made for. Matte paint, vinyl wraps and paint protection film need coatings made for them, with no added shine and no polishing first.

That is why I don't stick to one maker. One bottle can't be the right answer for every vehicle that pulls into a driveway.

The four questions I ask

  1. Where is it parked? A garage, a driveway under sprinklers, or a lot in full sun.
  2. How is it washed? By hand at home, at an automatic wash, or rarely.
  3. What does it go through? Winter salt and mag chloride, trails and brush, job-site dust.
  4. How long will you keep it? A lease ending next year and a truck you'll drive for a decade call for different coatings.

I rinse a panel at the walkaround to see how the paint sheds water today, and I measure paint depth before any polishing.3

By vehicle

Hunting & off-road rigs
A two-layer coating for chemical resistance, so mud and dust rinse off instead of baking on. Rock chips need paint protection film; a coating won't stop them.
Family rigs
A slick, hydrophobic coating, so water beads and rolls off and most of the mess comes off with a rinse.
Show cars
Paint correction first for depth and clarity, then a gloss-focused coating to keep it. It helps resist the light swirls washing adds, but it isn't scratch-proof.
Daily drivers in salt season
Chemical resistance against road salt and mag chloride, so winter grime washes off more easily.
Leases & first coatings
An easy-care single layer at the lowest coating price, sized to how long you'll keep the car.
Matte, wraps & PPF
A coating its maker rates for that finish. No polishing, and no added shine on matte.

What a coating won't do

  • It won't stop rock chips or deep scratches. That's what paint protection film is for.
  • It won't hide swirls. A coating locks in whatever is under it, so the paint is polished first.1
  • It doesn't mean you never wash again. It makes washing easier, and it still needs the right care to reach its rated life.

How the price works

A coating on gloss paint is the coating plus the correction grade it goes over. On a sedan, coatings start at $500 for the coating, plus Grade I correction from $399. I confirm the choice and the price with you at a free inspection, and nothing is added without your OK.

Questions

Does a more expensive coating always last longer?

Not always. Rated life, layers and what the coating is tuned for all change the price. A gloss-focused coating can cost more than a tougher one with a similar rating. That's why I pick by how you use the vehicle, not by price alone.

Can you put a coating over swirls?

You can, but the coating locks the swirls in. The paint is always polished first, and paint depth is measured on every panel so only what's needed comes off.

Where do you apply it?

At your place. In your garage when you have one, so the coating cures out of the sun, wind and dust. No garage? I use coatings made for outdoor application and plan the day around the weather. I tell you the cure times before I hand back the keys.
