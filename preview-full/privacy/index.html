Privacy

Your information, plainly.

What this site collects, why, and who else handles it. Last updated October 15, 2026.

What I collect, and when

  • Your quote and booking details. The quote builder runs in your browser. Your answers and vehicle are saved only in your own browser (so a reload doesn't lose them) until you choose to send them. When you tap to text or email me, your phone or email app sends what you see — your answers, name, number, address, photos — to me. I use it only to quote, book and do your job.
  • Visit counts (analytics). This site uses Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies, and GoatCounter, which doesn't, to count visits and see which pages and buttons get used (for example, "tapped Book"). Google Analytics collects things like your approximate location, device and browser, and the pages you view; it does not get your quote answers.
  • Payments. If you pay a deposit or a bill by card, Square processes the payment. I don't see or store your full card number.
  • The basics of any website. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the website host keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser) for security and reliability.

How I use it

To quote, schedule and do your job, text or email you about it, keep job records (including before-and-after photos), and see how the website is used so I can improve it. I don't sell your information, and I don't share it with anyone except the services named on this page that run the site and take payments.

Your choices

  • Opt out of Google Analytics: block or clear cookies for this site in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout (opens in a new tab)).
  • Clear a saved quote: clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Questions, a copy of what I have, or deletion: email johnny@lumenfinishworks.com. I'll delete what I hold about you unless I need to keep a record for taxes or an open job, and I'll tell you if that's the case.

Changes

If this changes, I'll update this page and its date. Lumen Finishworks LLC, Salt Lake Valley, Utah.
