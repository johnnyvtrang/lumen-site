Utah extras

Utah winters & trail days

Undercarriage flushes

Utah roads get salt and magnesium chloride all winter, and it sits where you can't see it — frame rails, brake lines, suspension. A normal wash never reaches it.

What you get with either flush

  • Degreaser worked into the frame rails, crossmembers, suspension, brake-line runs and wheel wells
  • A thorough rinse that flushes salt, magnesium chloride, mud and road grime out of the seams underneath
  • Leaves some anti-corrosion protection on the metal underneath, to help slow rust

Winter Salt Flush from $45
For road salt and mag chloride — after storms and at the end of salt season.

Trail Flush from $65
For mud and dust after off-road days, with extra time on skid plates and suspension. Mud packed solid +$50.

Available year-round — book one whenever you want it.

Questions

Before you book.

Raised on engines, driven by science: I’m happy to explain exactly what goes on your car and why.

Do I need to be home?

No. For mobile jobs, leave the key in a lockbox or another secure spot we agree on when you book, and make sure I can reach water and power. Please take valuables out or lock them away. I'll text when I arrive and when I'm done, with photos.

How long will it take?

Your quote shows the time for your exact vehicle. On a sedan, a Clean takes about 1 hour inside or out, and a Full Radiance (Clean, Restore & Protect) takes about 6 hours. Bigger vehicles take longer. Jobs over about 7.5 hours are split over two visits on back-to-back days. Paint correction and ceramic coatings are booked as their own full day or two.

Ceramic soap, ceramic spray, ceramic coating — what's the difference?

They're different products, each a step up. Ceramic soap (every Reflect (Clean & Protect) detail; I use Gtechniq W3 Ceramic GWash) is a ceramic-infused wash that leaves a slick, water-beading layer for up to 3 months (the maker's rating). Ceramic spray (Radiance (Clean, Restore & Protect)) is a basic hand-applied spray after the paint is decontaminated: Gtechniq C2, rated by its maker for up to 6 months, or Cerakote Platinum Rapid Ceramic, rated for 50 washes. Radiance+ upgrades any detail to a spray coating — Gyeon Q² CanCoat EVO (up to 12 months / 12,000 km) or Adam's Graphene Ceramic Spray Coating Advanced (rated 12–18 months). All three are light layers that are easy to renew at your next detail. A ceramic coating (Gyeon, Gtechniq or Cerakote; you choose) is a much harder, semi-permanent layer that bonds to the surface and lasts years. Because it locks in whatever is underneath, the paint is polished first. Paint coatings are available now if you have a garage I can work in (find your coating); glass, wheel and tire coatings can be added to any visit. Your quote always says which one you're getting.

Can you clean my kid's car seat?

Yes. One child car seat is included with every Interior or Full Reflect (Clean & Protect), and two with Radiance (Clean, Restore & Protect); any others (or any at Clean) are $35 each. Crumbs and spills are vacuumed out, the shell and buckles are cleaned, and the fabric cover is cleaned following the seat manufacturer's instructions. Harness straps are surface-cleaned with mild soap and water only, never steamed or soaked, because that can weaken the webbing. I'll take the seat out to clean underneath it, but for safety please reinstall it yourself (or have a certified car seat technician check it).
