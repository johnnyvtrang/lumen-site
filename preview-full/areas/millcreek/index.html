Local notes

Detailing in Millcreek.

What I see most on cars here, and what I'd do about it.

  1. 01

    Canyon trips and bike racks

    Trailhead parking up Millcreek Canyon means dusty paint, muddy wheel wells and bike racks that rarely get cleaned. Gear can be one of the included extras (1 at Renew, 2 at Radiance): a rack, box, rooftop tent or running boards cleaned and UV-protected in place.

  2. 02

    Sprinkler spots

    Cars parked next to lawns collect sprinkler overspray all summer, and Utah's hard water leaves mineral spots. Light spots on the glass are part of Renew; spots on the paint come off with a mineral remover before they have a chance to etch.

  3. 03

    Parking under trees

    Trees mean sap, pollen and bird droppings. Those are acidic and can mark clear coat if they sit; a ceramic layer gives them less to grab onto.

Questions

Millcreek questions.

Do you serve Olympus Cove and the upper east bench?

Yes, all of Millcreek is in the core valley with no travel fee. Steep driveways are fine; I just need a reasonably level spot to work around the car.

How often should I book if I park under trees?

Sap and droppings etch paint if they sit, so wash them off within a few days. How often is up to you.
