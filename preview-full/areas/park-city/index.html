Local notes

Detailing in Park City.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    Sun at 7,155 feet

    Park City's reference point is about 2,180 meters up, nearly 900 meters above Salt Lake City's at 4,223 feet.1 With UV climbing roughly 12% per 1,000 meters,3 that is about a quarter more than at sea level and around 11% more than Salt Lake City. Polycarbonate lenses and black trim pay for it first. Our restorations here finish with a ceramic UV coating under a 1-year clarity warranty, or, at Grade III, a sprayed 2K clear under three years, done in your garage.

    Headlight restoration

  2. 02

    Eight sampling areas

    Park City tests hardness each quarter in eight areas: Iron Canyon, Park Meadows, Fairway Hills, Prospector, Old Town, Lower and Upper Deer Valley, and Thaynes. From late 2025 to fall 2026, results ran 161 to 415 mg/L, 9 to 24 grains per gallon, and most fell between 17 and 22.2 Water like that marks glass wherever a drop dries. After the spots come off, a windshield or all-glass ceramic coating makes the next ones easier to lift.

    Glass & wheel coatings

  3. 03

    Even days, odd days

    From May 1 to September 30, Park City's water report asks even-numbered addresses to water on even-numbered days and odd on odd, and it notes that hardness shifts with the season and with which sources are in use.4 If your sprinklers reach the driveway, park clear on your days. Paint that already shows rings gets a mineral remover and clay first; a ceramic coating can follow, and it lifts the next rings with less effort.

    Paint correction & ceramic coating

Questions

Park City questions.

How much is travel to Park City?

A flat $50 per trip, because Park City is in our Regional zone. Lumen members pay half.

Do you work in Park City in winter?

Yes, year-round. Flushes and full exterior washes wait for an above-freezing day; interiors and headlight restoration in a garage don't. If the forecast turns, we move your visit at no fee.

Can you work on a car at a second home while I'm away?

Yes. Interiors and headlights fit most garages, and we agree on a secure spot for the key beforehand.
