Local notes

Detailing in Kaysville.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    April 15 to October 15

    Benchland, the Davis and Weber Canal Company and Haights Creek each run their own secondary system in Kaysville, and the water "is not treated like drinking water." Service starts about April 15, and winterizing begins October 15.2 Between those dates, a car parked by the lawn collects overspray. The spots are easiest to lift in their first weeks, so early summer or the week after shut-off is a good time for removal (from $50).

    Glass & wheel coatings

  2. 02

    The east-wind stretch

    During one high wind warning, the National Weather Service singled out the I-15 and US-89 corridors between Farmington and South Weber, and the Highway Patrol restricted high-profile vehicles on I-15, Legacy and US-89 in Davis County.4 Kaysville lies on that stretch, between Farmington and Layton.5 The morning after, grit sits in cowls, jambs and seals. Renew cleans the jambs and sills, and a blower dry clears the seams before anything touches the paint.

    Mobile detailing

  3. 03

    Weber Basin water at the hose

    The same Weber Basin water comes out of your hose bib, rated at 10 to 18 grains per gallon.3 Rinse a car with it in July sun and it rings as it dries, which is why we keep panels wet and blower-dry as we go. Between visits, Radiance's hand-applied ceramic spray layer helps the paint shed that water; a ceramic coating does the same for years, applied wherever the maker's temperature and humidity limits are met that day.

    Paint correction & ceramic coating

Questions

Kaysville questions.

Is there a travel fee to Kaysville?

Yes. Kaysville is in our Extended zone: a flat $25 per trip, however many vehicles we do. Lumen members pay half.

Does it matter which secondary-water company I'm on?

Not for the spots. Each company sets its own rules and schedule, but none of the water is treated like drinking water, so overspray leaves minerals either way.

Can a coating go on without paint correction?

Yes. It always goes on after a full wash, decontamination, clay and a panel wipe, because it seals in whatever is on the paint. Mobile or drop-off, by arrangement.
