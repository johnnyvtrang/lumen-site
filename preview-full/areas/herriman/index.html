Local notes

Detailing in Herriman.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    Wells that need blending

    Three of Herriman's groundwater sources run above 1,000 mg/L of dissolved solids, so the city mixes them with Jordan Valley water, which averages about 250.1 Even blended, a drop drying on the windshield leaves a visible ring. Fresh rings lift chemically (from $50); rings baked on through a summer take a stronger remover and clay, and any that have etched the glass are polished only after a test spot and a quote.

    Glass & wheel coatings

  2. 02

    Winter runs on Jordan Valley water

    Every year in mid-October Herriman moves to Jordan Valley water alone for the off-peak season, and the city notes that residents may see fewer mineral deposits and water spots.1 That makes late fall and winter a sensible window to strip the summer's spots and coat the paint. A coating always goes on after a full wash, decon, clay and panel wipe: outdoors when the maker allows it and the day suits, otherwise in a garage or covered space.

    Paint correction & ceramic coating

  3. 03

    Lenses at nearly 5,000 feet

    Herriman's Census reference point sits at 4,985 feet, about 1,520 meters up.2 At roughly 12% more UV for each 1,000 meters of altitude,3 that is close to a fifth more than at the coast, and headlight lenses yellow under it. Headlight restoration, Grades I and II, is done in the driveway and finished with a ceramic UV coating and a 1-year clarity warranty. Grade III adds a sprayed 2K clear and a 3-year warranty, and needs a garage.

Questions

Herriman questions.

Why is there a travel fee to Herriman?

It's outside our core valley, so a flat $25 covers the extra drive. It's charged once per trip, and Lumen members pay half.

Which water is my Herriman home on right now?

From mid-October through the off-peak season, Jordan Valley water only; in the warm months, a blend with the city's wells. Herriman's water quality coordinator can tell you the typical hardness at your address.

Will a coating stop Herriman water from spotting?

No coating makes water spot-free. It gives the minerals a slick surface to sit on instead of bare paint or glass, so they lift with less effort and are less likely to etch if you catch them early.
