Local notes

Detailing in Eagle Mountain.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    Dust from building sites

    In September 2026, residents of a subdivision north of Airport Road told KUTV that dust from a neighboring construction site had been coating their yards since early July, and the city said its environmental compliance officer had looked into the complaints.2 Fine dust scratches paint when it's wiped off dry. We snow-foam and pre-rinse before any mitt touches the car, and a ceramic coating lets the next film rinse away with less contact.

    Paint correction & ceramic coating

  2. 02

    Water up to 1,320 ppm

    The city draws on five wells and the Central Utah Water Conservancy District, and its 2025 report puts dissolved solids anywhere from 148 to 1,320 ppm, with sodium up to 182.1 Water near the top of that range leaves a heavy ring when it dries. If your spigot is nearby we'll use it and dry every panel by blower and towel; spots already on the glass get a test patch before we pick the remover.

    Glass & wheel coatings

  3. 03

    SR-73 and the corridor

    Since December 2025 the Mountain View Corridor has run from I-80 in Salt Lake County to SR-73 in Utah County,3 the state route that crosses Eagle Mountain.4 A commute on treated winter pavement leaves salt where a car wash never reaches: crossmembers, suspension arms, the inside of the wheel wells. We degrease and rinse those on an above-freezing day, and the flush finishes with a light anti-corrosion protection on the metal.

    Winter Salt & Trail Flushes

Questions

Eagle Mountain questions.

Why is the travel fee higher in Eagle Mountain?

It's farther out, in our Regional zone, so the flat fee is $50 per trip. Lumen members pay half, and a multi-vehicle trip shares it.

Do you detail UTVs and campers?

Yes. See Powersports for UTVs, dirt bikes, boats, RVs and trailers; on the same trip as a car, each additional vehicle is 15% off.

Will a coating keep construction dust off?

No coating keeps dust from landing. It gives the dust less to grip, so a gentle rinse and foam lift more of it before anything touches the paint.
