Local notes

Detailing in Draper.

What I see most on cars here, and what I'd do about it.

  1. 01

    Wind and dust

    The Point of the Mountain is windy enough to be one of Utah's best-known paragliding sites, and wind carries dust into every seam. A two-bucket hand wash and a blower dry keep it from being dragged across the paint.

  2. 02

    Trail days in Corner Canyon

    Mud-caked bikes and wheel wells? A Trail Flush clears packed mud from skid plates, suspension and wheel wells before it dries hard and traps moisture.

  3. 03

    New cars, protected early

    A new car is the best time for paint protection: a Gloss Enhancement first, then a multi-year ceramic coating in your garage.
