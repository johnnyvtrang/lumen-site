Local notes

Detailing in West Valley City.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    SR-201, I-215, Bangerter and Mountain View

    Mountain View Corridor reached SR-201 in West Valley City in 2021,1 joining I-215 and Bangerter Highway, which also run through the city.2 Highway miles embed brake dust in the paint as fine iron particles you can feel as grit after a wash. Renew dissolves them with an iron remover on the paint, faces and inner barrels, lifts tar from the rockers, and finishes with a ceramic soap layer.

    Mobile detailing

  2. 02

    Granger-Hunter water, about 10 grains

    Granger-Hunter Improvement District serves roughly 2100 to 4700 South, from 1300 to 7200 West,3 most of the city. Six of its own wells supply about a quarter of the water and Jordan Valley the rest, and its 2026 report puts average hardness at about 176 mg/L, about 10 grains per gallon.4 We blower-dry the seams and towel-dry the panels so none of it dries on the glass.

    Glass & wheel coatings

  3. 03

    Wheels between visits

    On a car that lives on those highways,2 the wheels gather brake dust fastest. Once they're decontaminated, a wheel ceramic coating keeps the next layer from bonding, so a rinse takes most of it. One option is rated by its maker up to 2 years and to 600 °C; a spray-on version for the faces has no maker rating and is simply renewed.

    Glass & wheel coatings

Questions

West Valley City questions.

Is there a travel fee in West Valley City?

No. All of West Valley City, Granger to Hunter, is in our core valley.

What is iron decontamination?

A remover that dissolves the brake-dust particles stuck in the paint and wheels, rinsed off before anything rubs the surface. It's part of Renew and Radiance.

How do I know which water district I'm on?

Your water bill names it. Granger-Hunter covers most of the city, but addresses outside its boundary are served by another district.

Do you detail work vans and trucks?

Yes. Businesses with four or more vehicles per visit can use Fleet Care on their lot, with no travel fee and one monthly invoice.
