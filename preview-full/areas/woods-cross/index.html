Local notes

Detailing in Woods Cross.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    Rail lines beside the parkway

    For much of its length, Legacy Parkway's east side roughly parallels the Union Pacific and UTA rail lines and I-15.1 Brake dust and rail fallout are both iron, and the particles lodge in clear coat; you feel them as grit on paint that looks clean. Renew's iron remover dissolves them chemically instead of scrubbing them across the finish, and the wheel faces and inner barrels get the same treatment.

    Mobile detailing

  2. 02

    After an east-wind night

    The National Weather Service issues high wind warnings for Davis County when downslope east winds come off the mountains, with gusts forecast to 65 mph or more in one warning.2 Wind like that packs grit into door jambs, wiper cowls and window seals. Renew cleans the jambs and sills, and a blower dry pushes grit out of the seams before a towel can drag it over the paint.

    Mobile detailing

  3. 03

    Hardness, borrowed from the neighbours

    We couldn't get Woods Cross's own water report, so this is not a Woods Cross measurement. It's the range Weber Basin, the wholesale supplier for neighboring Davis County cities, published for its water in 2025: 10 to 18 grains per gallon, or 171 to 308 ppm.3 Water anywhere in that range leaves rings when a sprinkler drop dries on glass, so we test one spot before picking a remover, and a windshield coating gives the next round less to hold.

Questions

Woods Cross questions.

Is there a travel fee to Woods Cross?

Yes, a flat $25 per trip, the same as Bountiful. Lumen members pay half.

Can you get baked-on brake dust off my wheels?

Usually. Iron remover dissolves most of it. If anything remains, it may have etched the finish, and we'll show you at the walkaround rather than promise it.

How hard is Woods Cross water?

We couldn't open the city's own water report, so we don't quote a Woods Cross number. The figure on this page is Weber Basin's published range for neighboring Davis County cities. We test a spot on your glass before choosing a remover, which matters more than the figure.
