Local notes

Detailing in Salt Lake City.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    Mountain water in spring, wells by August

    The city's three treatment plants, Big Cottonwood, Parleys and City Creek, measured 136, 175 and 212 mg/L total hardness in 2025. When stream runoff falls off in summer, the city blends in groundwater, and its wells and springs ran from 154 to 571 mg/L.2,3 A sprinkler drop that dries on a windshield in July can carry far more mineral than one in May. After the spots are removed, a glass ceramic coating, rated by its maker up to 24 months, helps water sheet off before it dries.

    Glass & wheel coatings

  2. 02

    Five or six inversions a winter

    Utah's Division of Air Quality counts about five to six multi-day inversions in a typical winter, when the mountains around the valley trap cold air, and the pollution in it, on the valley floor.4 A car parked through one comes out with a dull film that a quick wash only smears across the glass. Renew lifts it with iron and tar removal and cleans every pane inside and out, which you notice most at night, against oncoming headlights.

    Mobile detailing

  3. 03

    Downtown stalls and shared garages

    Downtown and Central Community each have their own city plan,1 and if your car lives in a shared garage or an assigned stall there, most of our work fits in the stall. Interiors, headlight restoration and rinseless washes need your building's OK and an outlet within about 50 feet, or we bring power. A full exterior wash needs somewhere for the runoff to go, so in a garage it becomes a drop-off.

    Mobile detailing

Questions

Salt Lake City questions.

Is there a travel fee anywhere in Salt Lake City?

No. The whole city, from the Avenues to the Westside, is in our core valley zone.

Why do my windows spot more in late summer?

Salt Lake City adds well water in summer, and its wells and springs test harder than its treatment plants. The same sprinkler leaves more behind. Light spots come off with a mineral remover; etched glass needs a polish, and we show you a test area first.

Can you detail my car in a downtown parking garage?

Often, for interiors, headlights and rinseless washes, once your building says yes. We use an outlet within about 50 feet if there is one and bring power if there isn't.

Do you work during an inversion?

Yes. We work year-round and the weather decides the day. Below freezing, we move the exterior work at no fee; interiors and glass can usually go ahead.
