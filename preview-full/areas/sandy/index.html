Local notes

Detailing in Sandy.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    Traction-law days on Little Cottonwood

    UDOT's traction law for Little Cottonwood Canyon calls for at least 5/32 inch of tread, and Sandy police are among the officers who enforce it.2 A day the law is in force is a storm day, and the car comes home carrying whatever the roads were treated with. A Winter Salt Flush degreases the frame rails, crossmembers and brake-line runs, then leaves a thin corrosion-inhibitor film. We book it for the next day above freezing, so the rinse can't freeze on the car.

    Winter Salt & Trail Flushes

  2. 02

    Surface water most of the year, wells in summer

    Sandy's treated surface water measured 158 mg/L total hardness in 2025, about 9 grains per gallon. The 16 wells that run mainly in summer aren't given a hardness figure in the report,1 so we don't guess one. Either way, sprinkler water that dries on glass or paint leaves its minerals behind. Light spots come off with a mineral remover (from $50); spots baked on by summer sun need a stronger remover and clay (from $100), and we test one spot first.

    Glass & wheel coatings

  3. 03

    Sun at about 4,800 feet

    Sandy sits at 4,786 feet at its Census reference point.3 UV rises about 12% for every 1,000 meters of altitude,4 so a car here takes roughly 17% more UV than one at sea level. Headlight lenses and black trim show it first. Headlight restoration takes about an hour in your driveway and carries a ceramic UV coating and a 1-year clarity warranty (Grade I from $99). Grade III, a sprayed 2K clear coat with a 3-year warranty, needs a garage.

    Headlight restoration

Questions

Sandy questions.

Is there a travel fee to Sandy?

No. Sandy is in our core valley zone, so a visit costs the same here as anywhere in the core valley.

Can you flush the salt off after a canyon day in January?

Yes, on the next day above freezing. We work through the winter and the weather decides the day; if a cold snap moves your visit, the new date costs nothing extra.

My car spots more in summer. Is that the wells?

Possibly. Sandy runs its wells mainly in summer and doesn't publish their hardness, so we can't say how much harder they are. The spots come off the same way; we test one before choosing the remover.
