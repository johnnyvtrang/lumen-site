Local notes

Detailing in Magna.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    Windy days by the tailings

    The Salt Lake Tribune reported in 2023 that on intensely windy days near Magna, dust from the Kennecott tailings can fade the view.1 Fine dust like that scratches if it's wiped off dry, so it comes off with foam and a rinse before a mitt touches the paint, and the jambs and seams get blown out. A hand-applied ceramic layer, or a ceramic coating, gives the next dusting less to cling to.

    Paint correction & ceramic coating

  2. 02

    Ten wells, 639 ppm

    Magna Water District draws from ten wells in two well fields, plus a Jordan Valley contract, and runs its own plant to reduce dissolved solids. Its 2023 report lists them at 639 ppm on average, 460 to 848, with hardness from 58 to 185 mg/L.2 That residue is what a drop leaves on glass. Light spots come off with a mineral remover; etched glass needs a polish, quoted first.

    Glass & wheel coatings

  3. 03

    SR-201 and I-80 out of town

    SR-201 runs along Magna's northern edge and meets I-80 to the west,3 so most trips start on a highway. In winter that means brine thrown up into the frame rails and brake-line runs. A Winter Salt Flush degreases the underside and leaves a corrosion-inhibitor film, booked for a day above freezing so the rinse runs off instead of icing.

    Winter Salt & Trail Flushes

Questions

Magna questions.

Why is Magna in the Extended zone?

It's past the west edge of our core valley, so a flat $25 covers the drive, once per trip. Lumen members pay half.

Can my neighbor and I share the travel fee?

Yes. Book the same visit and it's one trip and one fee, with 15% off every additional vehicle.

Why do my windows spot so badly?

Magna's well water carries a lot of dissolved solids, 639 ppm on average in 2023. Drops that dry in the sun leave them behind. We test a spot and quote any polishing before doing it.
