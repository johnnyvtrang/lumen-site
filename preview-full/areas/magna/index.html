Local notes

Detailing in Magna.

What I see most on cars here, and what I'd do about it.

  1. 01

    Wind and dust

    A windy week means dusty paint and gritty door jambs. Renew cleans the jambs and sills, and a blower dry keeps grit out of the seams.

  2. 02

    Older trucks and classics

    Faded black trim and cloudy headlights are the two quickest ways to make an older vehicle look newer. Both are restored, not dressed over.

  3. 03

    Share the trip

    Book with a neighbor for the same visit and you pay one travel fee between you, with 15% off every additional vehicle.
