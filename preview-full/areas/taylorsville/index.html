Local notes

Detailing in Taylorsville.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    Well water, up to 976 ppm

    Taylorsville-Bennion Improvement District runs 11 wells and buys the rest of its water from Jordan Valley. Its 2024 report lists total dissolved solids averaging 504.7 ppm, from 268 up to 976 ppm by source.1 That is what stays behind on the paint when a drop dries. Caught early, it's a deposit and a mineral remover lifts it. Left through a summer, a ring can cut into the clear coat, and then only polishing removes it.

    Paint correction & ceramic coating

  2. 02

    Etch is measured in microns

    Ford researchers describe etch as what looks like water spotting that won't come off. The pits an owner can see were about 3 to 5 microns deep, and their middle severity band was rated repairable by polishing.2 That's the band Grade I or II correction is for: we measure the paint, polish a test section, and show you the result before doing the rest. Deeper etch is a refinish, and we'll say so.

    Paint correction & ceramic coating

  3. 03

    The windshield gets it too

    The same water lands on glass, and the dissolved solids in it1 dry into rings that the wipers then drag around. Light rings come off with a mineral remover; etched glass takes a windshield polish. After that, a glass ceramic coating helps rain and sprinkler water sheet off before it dries.

Questions

Taylorsville questions.

Is there a travel fee to Taylorsville?

No. Taylorsville is inside the core valley.

Do I need correction, or just a spot remover?

If the spots come off with a remover and clay, that's all you need. If a dull ring stays under a light afterwards, it's etched, and correction is the fix. We test one area and show you before quoting.

Can correction happen in my driveway?

Grades I and II, yes, in your driveway or garage. Grade III takes up to 2 days, so it's a drop-off.

If I skip correction, can I still get a coating?

Yes, correction is optional. Prep isn't: wash, decon, clay and a panel wipe come first. Any etched ring left under it stays visible, sealed in, so we show you the test section before you decide.
