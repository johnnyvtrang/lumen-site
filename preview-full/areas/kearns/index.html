Local notes

Detailing in Kearns.

What we see most on cars here, and what we do about it.

  1. 01

    The Oval's thin air, on your paint

    The Oval is known for its altitude,1 and Kearns sits at about 4,680 feet at its Census reference point.2 Ultraviolet rises by about 12% per 1,000 meters,3 so paint and trim parked here take noticeably more of it than at the coast. A ceramic coating puts a hard layer over the clear coat, and black trim gets a trim coating rated for UV. A coating needs no correction first, but it always follows a full wash, decon, clay and panel wipe.

    Paint correction & ceramic coating

  2. 02

    94% Jordan Valley water

    Kearns Improvement District buys 94% of its water from Jordan Valley,4 whose total hardness ran from 75.6 to 357 mg/L across its sources in 2023.5 Water at the upper end leaves heavy rings when it dries on warm glass. We dry by blower and towel, take off what's there with a mineral remover, and a glass coating makes the next sprinkler pass bead and roll.

    Glass & wheel coatings

  3. 03

    Twelve Kearns wells

    The other 6% comes from 12 district wells in Kearns, with total dissolved solids up to 684 ppm in the district's 2022 samples.4 On a car washed between visits with that water, a fresh ceramic layer matters. A Lumen Membership renews the ceramic soap layer on every third, deeper visit, so a home rinse between visits leaves less behind.

    Lumen Membership
