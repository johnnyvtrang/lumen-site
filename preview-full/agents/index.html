Offer 1 · Closing gifts

The Closing Gift Card: any amount works.

Any amount works, from $25, as credit toward any service. $150 or more adds one add-on of their choice; $300 or more adds two. Each add-on is complimentary, up to a $60 value, picked from:

  • Light pet hair removal ($20) or heavy pet hair ($40)
  • Child car seat deep clean ($35)
  • Winter Salt Flush ($50)
  • Odor treatment, ozone ($55)
  • Engine bay detail ($60)

Add-on prices are for a sedan. On a larger vehicle, each add-on is covered up to $60 and your client pays the difference.

Who it fits:

  • Families with kids. Car seats, crumbs and sticky spots, cleaned the week they move in.
  • Pet owners. The pet hair from the move, gone.
  • A car they baby. The paint is gauged before any machine polishing, the products are chosen for the finish, and coated, PPF and matte cars get the care their finish needs.
  • Anyone who just moved. Boxes, dust and moving grime out of the car. One thing off their plate.

Offer 2 · Your own car

Your first membership visit: Radiance, for the Renew price.

Start any Lumen Membership and your first visit is a Full Radiance billed at the Full Renew price: $229 instead of $299 for a sedan. I come to your office while you work, or to your home.

  • Radiance outside: a full decon, clay, faded trim restored and a hand-applied ceramic sealant (maker-rated up to 6 months).
  • Radiance inside: fully steamed, with the leather conditioned.
  • Inspected under detailing lights, with before & after photos sent to you.

After that first visit, regular membership prices apply. For a sedan: every 2 weeks $139 a visit (saves 20%), monthly $149 (saves 15%), every 2 months $155 (saves 10%) or quarterly $215 (saves 5%), against booking the same visits one at a time.

Terms. The Radiance upgrade applies to the first visit of a membership with a 3-visit minimum: if you stop before your third visit, the upgrade difference (+$70 on a sedan) is billed. It's open to the first 10 agents who start a membership. Otherwise the usual membership terms apply: billed per visit, no contract, no cancellation fee. How the membership works.

Larger vehicles have their own prices. Text me your year, make and model and I'll send the first-visit price for your car.

Questions

Agent questions.

Can I gift any amount?

Yes, any amount from $25 works as credit toward any service. $150 or more adds one complimentary add-on of their choice; $300 or more adds two (each up to $60). You can buy several cards in one order, and I can tell you about physical cards if you ask.

Do the gift cards expire?

No. They're Square eGift cards, and Square gift cards have no expiration date. The add-ons go with the card.

How do you know it's a closing gift?

Put "Closing gift" and your name in the gift message when you buy it, or text me. Your client just mentions it when they book. It's on the honor system.

Will you try to sell my client more?

No. Your client books whatever they want, at the price the quote builder shows for their vehicle, picks any complimentary add-ons their card includes, and I don't offer them anything else. If the detail they pick costs more than the card, they see the price before anything is booked.

Can you detail my car at my office?

Yes, anywhere I work in the Salt Lake Valley. Mobile visits go best with an outdoor spigot and an outlet within about 50 ft, and I carry water and power as a backup. Please check with your building or office manager that detailing in the lot is OK. Drop-off works too, with no travel fee.

What if I stop the membership?

You can stop any time, with no cancellation fee. The agent offer has a 3-visit minimum: if you stop before your third visit, the Radiance upgrade difference (+$70 on a sedan) is billed. Membership details.

My car has a ceramic coating, PPF or a wrap. Does the offer apply?

Those finishes get the Protected membership, which starts with a Protected Full Detail instead of a Radiance. Text me first and I'll confirm the agent first-visit price for your car before you book.

Is this a referral arrangement?

No. I don't pay referral fees, and no price depends on who you send my way. Some brokerages have their own rules about vendor offers, so please check yours.
