Questions

Partner questions.

Which details does the perk apply to?

Any detail that includes the interior: an Interior or Full detail, at any level. One perk per detail, and it has no cash value. On Renew and Radiance, light pet hair is already one of the included picks, so the perk frees that pick for something else, like a child car seat or a piece of gear.

What if the dog hair is heavy?

Hair worked into the seams and carpet is heavy pet hair, its own add-on. I price it from a photo at booking, like any job, so your client sees the price before anything is booked. The perk covers light pet hair.

How do you know a client came from us?

Your business gets its own code, BARK- and your name. Your client mentions your business or gives the code when they book, and I note it on the booking. It's on the honor system.

Do you pay commissions or referral fees?

No. There are no commissions or referral fees, and there is nothing for you to sign up for or run. The perk goes to your clients, and your staff get the same perk when they mention your business.

Can you drop off cards for our front desk?

Yes, I can drop off cards. Each has a QR code your clients can scan to get a price for their own vehicle. Text me and we'll find a time that works.

Do you detail pet transport vans?

Yes. Van detailing is quoted after I see one van, because pickup and transport vans vary a lot. Text me about your vans.

What do you use inside, with pets in mind?

Here's exactly what I do, so your clients can decide. Interior cleaners are wiped off with a clean, damp towel; fabric is shampooed with pH-balanced cleaners, then extracted, which pulls the cleaner back out. It's fragrance-free by default. Before I hand back the keys, I say how long to keep pets off (shampooed fabric usually 2–6 hours), and Safety Data Sheets for every product I use are available on request. More on kids & pets.
