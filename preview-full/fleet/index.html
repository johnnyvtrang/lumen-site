Fleet Care — as needed

from $45 / vehicle

Each vehicle is billed at the fleet price below for the service it got.

Text me about Fleet Care
  • Each visit I look over every vehicle and do only what it needs: a Fleet Wash, Fleet Maintenance, or a Deep Clean when it's due.
  • Less needed means less to do — and less to charge. You pay the fleet price for what was done, nothing for work a vehicle didn't need.
  • In winter: road-salt rinses and undercarriage degreasing flushes when vehicles need them. Interiors cleaned only when they need it.
  • Per-vehicle photo log showing what was done and why.
  • Budget control: anything beyond Fleet Maintenance (a Deep Clean or add-ons) is done only after a quick text OK — or set a monthly cap and I'll stay under it.
  • Same terms as other fleet service: 4+ vehicles per visit, no travel fee, monthly invoice Net 15, 10% off Wash and Maintenance at 10+ vehicles.
