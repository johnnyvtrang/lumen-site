FAQ

Mobile detailing FAQ.

Questions, answered. The short answers on visits, weather, timing, payment and products. Don't see yours? Text us.

Visits & timing

Do I need to be home?

Not for the whole visit. Either works for the key: hand it to us when we arrive, or leave it in a lockbox or another secure spot we agree on when you book. Please stay reachable by phone, and take valuables out or lock them away. If you have an outdoor spigot and outlet nearby, we'll use them; if not, we bring our own spot-free water and power. We'll text when we arrive and when we're done, with photos.

Do you bring your own water and power?

Yes. If you have an outdoor spigot and outlet nearby, we'll use them; if not, we bring our own spot-free water and power. Using yours keeps our onboard water for more jobs. Prefer to drop the car off instead? Drop-off is available by arrangement, with no travel fee.

Where do you work? Can I drop my car off?

Mobile or drop-off, your choice. Mobile: we come to your home or workplace (fleets are always on your lot). Drop-off: you bring the vehicle to us, with no travel fee; the drop-off location is shared when you book. In the quote builder, choose mobile and mention drop-off when you text. Studio opening 2027.

What if the weather turns?

We watch the forecast. If rain, snow or freezing temperatures would hurt the result, we'll text you the evening before. You can move the appointment to the next open day. Weather changes never cost you a fee. Headlight-only jobs can often still go ahead if the car is in a garage or carport. We work year-round: every service is available in winter too, and when the day doesn't suit, we use a garage or covered space if one is available.

How long will it take?

Your quote shows the time for your exact vehicle. On a sedan, a Refresh takes about 1 hour inside or out, and a Full Radiance takes about 6 hours. Bigger vehicles take longer. Jobs over about 7.5 hours are split over two visits on back-to-back days. Paint correction and ceramic coatings are booked as their own full day or two.

When will you arrive?

We arrive within 15 minutes of the scheduled time. If anything changes, we tell you in advance, with a new time, before your window starts. On the day, we text when we're on our way.

Can you come today, or on a weekend?

Yes, same-day and weekend jobs, when the schedule allows. Text us to check what's open.

Pricing & payment

How do I pay? Is there a deposit?

Yes — a deposit of 25% of your quote, rounded to the nearest $5 ($25 minimum), holds your date, and it comes off your final bill. Once we've picked a time, we'll text you a secure payment link to hold your date. Cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours ahead and your deposit moves to the new date or is refunded; later cancellations and no-shows keep it. If we call off a visit for weather, your deposit always carries over. The rest is due when the work is done — we take major credit and debit cards. Fleet accounts are invoiced monthly, with no deposit. Prices are before Utah sales tax where it applies.

Will you try to upsell me?

No. You get your price up front, itemized for your vehicle, before you book. At the walkaround, if something on your quote isn't needed — or is only partly needed — we take it off or discount it. If we find something you didn't mention, we show you and tell you the price, and nothing is added unless you say yes.

Will my price change after I book?

Your price is quoted up front for your vehicle and confirmed at the walkaround, before we start. Nothing is added without your yes. If the job turns out different from your photos or description, we show you and tell you the price before we start, and you can say no: we do the job as booked. Anything on your quote that isn't needed comes off.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back and make it right.

What if you miss something I paid for?

Before we leave, we walk the car with you and go through every line you paid for, one by one, with photos of each in your job report. If you're not home, we text you the photos. If something was missed or isn't right, tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back and make it right, at no charge.

Is there a travel fee?

Not in the core valley, from Salt Lake City to Draper and from Millcreek and Holladay to West Jordan. Beyond it there's one flat fee per trip: $25 in the Extended zone (for example Bluffdale, Bountiful, Farmington, Herriman, Lehi, Magna and Saratoga Springs) and $50 in the Regional zone (for example Park City and Eagle Mountain). Lumen members pay half. See every city and its zone.

Is there a discount for more than one vehicle?

Yes. More than one vehicle on the same trip, at the same address or neighbors who book together for the same visit: the highest-priced one is full price and every additional vehicle is 15% off, whether it's a car, truck, SUV, van, powersports machine, boat or RV.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes: digital gift cards in any amount, delivered by email or text and usable toward any service. Order a gift card (opens in a new tab), or see how gift cards work.

Services & products

Wax soap, ceramic soap, ceramic sealant, coating — what's the difference?

They're different products, each a step up. Wax soap (Refresh) washes and leaves a little gloss and water beading between visits. Ceramic soap (Renew) is a ceramic-infused wash that leaves a slick, water-beading layer its maker rates for up to 3 months. Ceramic sealant (Radiance) goes on by hand, panel by panel, after a full decon and the ceramic soap wash — maker-rated up to 6 months or 50 washes. Halo upgrades any detail to a spray coating, maker-rated up to 12–18 months. All of these are light layers that are easy to renew at your next detail. A ceramic coating (you choose the level) is a much harder, semi-permanent layer that bonds to the surface and lasts years. It seals in the paint as it is, so swirls or scratches stay visible under it; correcting the paint first removes them, and is optional. Paint coatings are available now, applied outdoors when the coating's maker allows it and the day's conditions fit, or in a garage or covered space when they don't (find your coating); glass, wheel and tire coatings can be added to any visit. Your quote always says which one you're getting.

Can you do paint correction and ceramic coating at my place?

Yes, for Grades I and II: they're done on-site, outside or in your garage; you don't need a garage for them. Grade III (two-step) can take up to 2 days, so it's drop-off only. Paint coatings go on outdoors when the coating's maker allows it and the day's conditions fit, or in a garage or covered space when they don't. Correction before a coating is optional. Wheels-off wheel coatings (quoted in person) need a garage. Glass, wheel-face and tire coatings can be added to any visit. Prefer to drop the car off? Drop-off is available by arrangement.

My car has a ceramic coating from another shop. Can you maintain it?

Yes. The Coated Care Wash and the Annual Coating Service are built for coated cars. After the wash we apply a ceramic topper: it refreshes the coating's slickness and water beading and adds a thin sacrificial layer that takes the wear instead of the coating — it's part of the wash, not an extra. Tell us who installed it — some coating warranties require an annual inspection by the original installer.

How do I know the coating was actually applied?

It's written down. Your job report records the coating product, its lot number, the number of layers, the date and the warranty end date, with the panel-by-panel paint-gauge readings and before & after photos. The warranty end date also goes on your receipt. See a sample job report.

Is it safe for my matte paint or wrap?

Yes. Matte-specific products only, no wax, gloss enhancers or polish, a test spot before anything new, and hand care at every film edge with the pressure washer kept well back. How we care for wrapped, matte and PPF cars.

Do you do PPF, tint or wraps?

Through our install partner, a local PPF, tint and wrap shop we work with. We handle the prep before the film goes on and the finish-safe aftercare after; they handle the film. You book and pay the install shop directly, and they quote the film work. How the partner service works, or text us about PPF, tint or wraps.

My car has Alcantara, chrome, carbon or a soft top. Is that extra?

Only when there's enough of it to need its own products and time. These are never part of a level — they cost the same whichever level you choose, and your quote shows the price for your vehicle. Large Alcantara or microsuede areas (seats and door inserts; an Alcantara headliner is part of Headliner cleaning) are from $40: a dedicated cleaner, almost no moisture, no steam or heat, and the nap brushed back up by hand. Chrome window trim, emblems and handles are from $59, polished by hand and sealed; a lot of chrome — grille, bumpers, mirror caps, running boards — is from $99, with the trim included. A convertible soft top is from $70: fabric is cleaned and reproofed, vinyl is cleaned and UV-protected. Exposed carbon fiber is from $49, cleaned and sealed with no polish or abrasives. Small pieces — a shift boot, steering-wheel trim, a carbon mirror cap, a few chrome emblems — are included at no charge. Genuine suede, nubuck, aniline leather and classic single-stage paint are quoted after a test spot, because water can mark those leathers and old paint can react to polish.

Is it safe for my kids and pets?

Here's exactly what we do, so you can decide. Outside, every acid or alkaline step is rinsed away before the pH-neutral wash and protection go on; inside, cleaners are wiped off with a clean, damp towel, so nothing harsh is left on the surfaces you touch. From Renew up, hard interior surfaces are steam-cleaned — just water and heat — and fabric is shampooed with pH-balanced cleaners, then extracted, which pulls the cleaner back out. Before we hand back the keys, we tell you the dry and cure times (shampooed fabric usually 2–6 hours; coatings per the maker's directions — a leather coating 6 hours before the seats are used, a fabric coating 24 hours); keep kids and pets off until then. It's fragrance-free by default, with no scented sprays unless you ask, and Safety Data Sheets for every product we use are available on request. Child car seats are cleaned only as the seat's maker allows: harness straps are surface-wiped with mild soap and water, never soaked or chemically treated.

What if I pick the wrong level or too much?

Not sure you picked right? Book your best guess. At the walkaround — before we start — we'll take off anything you don't need, and nothing gets added without your OK. Your 25% deposit simply comes off the final total, whatever it ends up being.

Can you clean my kid's car seat?

Yes. Renew includes one extra you choose and Radiance two — light pet hair, a child car seat or a piece of gear — so a child car seat can be one of them (on Interior or Full details; Radiance could be two car seats). Any other seat is $35, at any level. Crumbs and spills are vacuumed out, the shell is wiped with mild soap and water, the buckles are rinsed with warm water only and click-tested, and the fabric cover is cleaned following the seat manufacturer's instructions. No disinfectants, protectants, coatings, fragrance or ozone ever go on a child seat. Harness straps are surface-cleaned with mild soap and water only, never steamed or soaked, because that can weaken the webbing. We'll take the seat out to clean underneath it, but for safety please reinstall it yourself (or have a certified car seat technician check it).

Is pet hair extra?

Light pet hair can be one of the extras Renew (one) and Radiance (two) include, chosen from light pet hair, a child car seat or a piece of gear. Otherwise light pet hair removal is $20 on a sedan, scaled to your vehicle. Heavy pet hair — a dog that rides often, with hair worked into the seams and carpet — is its own add-on, from $40, at every level. Your quote shows which extras are included.

Why not just use a headlight kit from the store?

Kits take off some haze, but without a multi-stage machine polish the lens is left finely scratched, so it clouds again sooner. We machine-correct the lens through three polishing stages, apply a ceramic UV coating, and back it with a 1-year clarity warranty — if it yellows or clouds within a year, we re-polish and re-coat it free.

Can you do a test spot first?

Yes — free, for any service. Before you commit, we'll try the product or the polish on a small area so you can see the result on your own car: a stain on your seat, a water spot, a hazy headlight, or a 50/50 section of polished paint. Then you decide.

Do you clean engine bays?

Yes. The engine bay detail is an add-on to any detail, $60 on a sedan; your quote shows the price for your vehicle. We work on a cool engine, cover the alternator, intake and fuse box, use a low-pressure rinse, blow it dry and finish the plastics and hoses with a dressing. We don't offer it on electric vehicles. Price it in the quote builder.

Five steps that are part of every job, at every level: a walkaround with photos before we start, a free test spot (always on delicate surfaces, and for any service on request), pH-safe chemistry, a final inspection under detailing lights, and before & after photos sent to you. Utah conditions

Why wash under the car?

Two reasons in Utah: winter salt and trail mud. Road salt and magnesium chloride collect on the frame, brake lines and suspension, where a normal wash never reaches. A Winter Salt Flush (from $50) is a thorough degreasing wash of everything underneath that lifts the salt and grime off and leaves some anti-corrosion protection behind — a cheap way to slow rust. After Moab or the dunes, the Trail Flush (from $70) is the same degreasing wash, built to clear packed mud from skid plates, suspension and wheel wells before it dries hard and traps moisture (mud packed solid +$50). Both are available year-round on above-freezing days, done over a containment mat that captures the run-off — it's collected and disposed of properly, never into a storm drain. More on the Winter Salt Flush page.

Can you clean up after the Salt Flats or a muddy trail?

Yes. Underneath, the Winter Salt Flush (from $50) lifts salt off the frame, brake lines and suspension, and the Trail Flush (from $70) clears mud from skid plates, suspension and wheel wells (mud packed solid +$50, confirmed at the walkaround). The paint, glass and wheels get an exterior detail. Both flushes are done on above-freezing days. If the vehicle is caked, text us a few photos and we'll quote it from the photos.

Can you remove water spots from sprinklers or hard water?

Usually, yes. Light spots sitting on the surface come off chemically (from $50). Spots baked on by the sun need a stronger mineral remover and clay (from $100). If rings are still there after that, the minerals have etched into the glass or clear coat and need machine polishing — we'll show you a test spot and quote it before going further. Afterward we seal the surface so spots don't bond as easily next time.
