FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short answers on visits, weather, timing, payment and products. Don't see yours? Text me.

Visits & timing

Do I need to be home?

No. For mobile jobs, leave the key in a lockbox or another secure spot we agree on when you book, and make sure I can reach water and power. Please take valuables out or lock them away. I'll text when I arrive and when I'm done, with photos.

Do you bring your own water and power?

Not yet. Mobile appointments need an outdoor spigot and an outlet within about 50 ft. Headlight-only jobs need just an outlet. If you don't have both within reach, choose drop-off instead: you bring the vehicle to me, so you don't need either, and there's no travel fee. Or text me before booking and I'll tell you what can work.

Where do you work? Can I drop my car off?

Mobile or drop-off, your choice. Mobile: I come to your home or workplace (fleets are always on your lot). Drop-off: you bring the vehicle to me, with no travel fee; the drop-off location is shared when you book. In the quote builder, choose mobile and mention drop-off when you text. A studio is coming soon.

What if the weather turns?

I watch the forecast. If rain, snow or freezing temperatures would hurt the result, I'll text you the evening before. You can move the appointment to the next open day. Weather changes never cost you a fee. Headlight-only jobs can often still go ahead if the car is in a garage or carport. From about November to March, mobile visits cover interiors, rinseless washes, headlights and undercarriage flushes; full exterior details resume in April.

How long will it take?

Your quote shows the time for your exact vehicle. On a sedan, a Refresh takes about 1 hour inside or out, and a Full Radiance takes about 6 hours. Bigger vehicles take longer. Jobs over about 7.5 hours are split over two visits on back-to-back days. Paint correction and ceramic coatings are booked as their own full day or two.

Pricing & payment

How do I pay? Is there a deposit?

Yes — a deposit of 25% of your quote, rounded to the nearest $5 ($25 minimum), holds your date, and it comes off your final bill. Once we've picked a time, I'll text you a secure payment link to hold your date. Cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours ahead and your deposit moves to the new date or is refunded; later cancellations and no-shows keep it. If I call off a visit for weather, your deposit always carries over. The rest is due when the work is done — I take major credit and debit cards. Fleet accounts are invoiced monthly, with no deposit. Prices are before Utah sales tax where it applies.

Will you try to upsell me?

No. You get your price up front, itemized for your vehicle, before you book. At the walkaround, if something on your quote isn't needed — or is only partly needed — I take it off or discount it. If I find something you didn't mention, I show you and tell you the price, and nothing is added unless you say yes.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell me within 48 hours and I'll come back and make it right.

Is there a travel fee?

Not in the core valley, from Salt Lake City to Draper and from Millcreek and Holladay to West Jordan. Beyond it there's one flat fee per trip: $25 in the Extended zone (for example Bountiful, Farmington, Herriman, Lehi, Magna and Saratoga Springs) and $50 in the Regional zone (for example Park City and Eagle Mountain). Lumen members pay half. See every city and its zone.

Is there a discount for more than one vehicle?

Yes. More than one vehicle on the same trip, at the same address or neighbors who book together for the same visit: the highest-priced one is full price and every additional vehicle is 15% off, whether it's a car, truck, SUV, van, powersports machine, boat or RV.

Services & products

Wax soap, ceramic soap, ceramic sealant, coating — what's the difference?

They're different products, each a step up. Wax soap (Refresh) washes and leaves a little gloss and water beading between visits. Ceramic soap (Renew) is a ceramic-infused wash that leaves a slick, water-beading layer its maker rates for up to 3 months. Ceramic sealant (Radiance) goes on by hand, panel by panel, after a full decon and the ceramic soap wash — maker-rated up to 6 months or 50 washes. Halo upgrades any detail to a spray coating, maker-rated up to 12–18 months. All of these are light layers that are easy to renew at your next detail. A ceramic coating (you choose the level) is a much harder, semi-permanent layer that bonds to the surface and lasts years. Because it locks in whatever is underneath, the paint is polished first. Paint coatings are available now if you have a garage I can work in (find your coating); glass, wheel and tire coatings can be added to any visit. Your quote always says which one you're getting.

Can you do paint correction and ceramic coating at my place?

Yes, for Grades I and II: they're done on-site, outside or in your garage; you don't need a garage for them. Grade III (two-step) can take up to 2 days, so it's drop-off only. Ceramic paint coatings and wheels-off wheel coatings (quoted in person) need a garage I can work in, so the coating goes on and cures out of the sun, wind and dust, and the paint is always polished first. Glass, wheel-face and tire coatings can be added to any visit. No garage? Drop-off is coming once I have a workspace.

My car has a ceramic coating from another shop. Can you maintain it?

Yes. Refresh Washes and Decon & Reseal are built for coated cars. Tell me who installed it — some coating warranties require an annual inspection by the original installer.

Is it safe for my matte paint or wrap?

That's the specialty. Matte-specific products only, no wax or gloss enhancers, and careful technique at every film edge.

Do you do PPF, tint or wraps?

Through my install partner, a local PPF, tint and wrap shop I work with. I handle the prep before the film goes on and the finish-safe aftercare after; they handle the film. You book and pay the install shop directly, and they quote the film work. Ask about bundles: tint with an Interior detail, headlight restoration with headlight PPF, or a chrome delete with wrap-safe aftercare. How the partner service works, or text me about PPF, tint or wraps.

My car has Alcantara, chrome, carbon or a soft top. Is that extra?

Only when there's enough of it to need its own products and time. These are never part of a level — they cost the same whichever level you choose, and your quote shows the price for your vehicle. Large Alcantara or microsuede areas (seats and door inserts; an Alcantara headliner is part of Headliner cleaning) are from $40: a dedicated cleaner, almost no moisture, no steam or heat, and the nap brushed back up by hand. Chrome window trim, emblems and handles are from $59, polished by hand and sealed; a lot of chrome — grille, bumpers, mirror caps, running boards — is from $99, with the trim included. A convertible soft top is from $70: fabric is cleaned and reproofed, vinyl is cleaned and UV-protected. Exposed carbon fiber is from $49, cleaned and sealed with no polish or abrasives. Small pieces — a shift boot, steering-wheel trim, a carbon mirror cap, a few chrome emblems — are included at no charge. Genuine suede, nubuck, aniline leather and classic single-stage paint are quoted after a test spot, because water can mark those leathers and old paint can react to polish.

Is it safe for my kids and pets?

Here's exactly what I do, so you can decide. Outside, every acid or alkaline step is rinsed away before the pH-neutral wash and protection go on; inside, cleaners are wiped off with a clean, damp towel, so nothing harsh is left on the surfaces you touch. From Renew up, hard interior surfaces are steam-cleaned — just water and heat — and fabric is shampooed with pH-balanced cleaners, then extracted, which pulls the cleaner back out. Before I hand back the keys, I tell you the dry and cure times (shampooed fabric usually 2–6 hours; coatings per the maker's directions — a leather coating 6 hours before the seats are used, a fabric coating 24 hours); keep kids and pets off until then. It's fragrance-free by default, with no scented sprays unless you ask, and Safety Data Sheets for every product I use are available on request. Child car seats are cleaned only as the seat's maker allows: harness straps are surface-wiped with mild soap and water, never soaked or chemically treated.

What if I pick the wrong level or too much?

Not sure you picked right? Book your best guess. At the walkaround — before I start — I'll take off anything you don't need, and nothing gets added without your OK. Your 25% deposit simply comes off the final total, whatever it ends up being.

Can you clean my kid's car seat?

Yes. Renew includes one extra you choose and Radiance two — light pet hair, a child car seat or a piece of gear — so a child car seat can be one of them (on Interior or Full details; Radiance could be two car seats). Any other seat is $35, at any level. Crumbs and spills are vacuumed out, the shell and buckles are cleaned, and the fabric cover is cleaned following the seat manufacturer's instructions. Harness straps are surface-cleaned with mild soap and water only, never steamed or soaked, because that can weaken the webbing. I'll take the seat out to clean underneath it, but for safety please reinstall it yourself (or have a certified car seat technician check it).

Is pet hair extra?

Light pet hair can be one of the extras Renew (one) and Radiance (two) include, chosen from light pet hair, a child car seat or a piece of gear. Otherwise light pet hair removal is $20 on a sedan, scaled to your vehicle. Heavy pet hair — a dog that rides often, with hair worked into the seams and carpet — is its own add-on, from $40, at every level. Your quote shows which extras are included.

Why not just use a headlight kit from the store?

Kits take off some haze, but without a multi-stage machine polish the lens is left finely scratched, so it clouds again sooner. I machine-correct the lens through three polishing stages, apply a ceramic UV coating, and back it with a 1-year clarity warranty — if it yellows or clouds within a year, I re-polish and re-coat it free.

Can you do a test spot first?

Yes — free, for any service. Before you commit, I'll try the product or the polish on a small area so you can see the result on your own car: a stain on your seat, a water spot, a hazy headlight, or a 50/50 section of polished paint. Then you decide.

What is the Lumen Standard?

Five steps that are part of every job, at every level: a walkaround with photos before I start, a free test spot (always on delicate surfaces, and for any service on request), pH-safe chemistry, a final inspection under detailing lights, and before & after photos sent to you. Utah conditions

Why wash under the car?

Two reasons in Utah: winter salt and trail mud. Road salt and magnesium chloride collect on the frame, brake lines and suspension, where a normal wash never reaches. A Winter Salt Flush (from $50) is a thorough degreasing wash of everything underneath that lifts the salt and grime off and leaves some anti-corrosion protection behind — a cheap way to slow rust. After Moab or the dunes, the Trail Flush (from $70) is the same degreasing wash, built to clear packed mud from skid plates, suspension and wheel wells before it dries hard and traps moisture (mud packed solid +$50). Both are available year-round — whenever you want one.

Can you remove water spots from sprinklers or hard water?

Usually, yes. Light spots sitting on the surface come off chemically (from $50). Spots baked on by the sun need a stronger mineral remover and clay (from $100). If rings are still there after that, the minerals have etched into the glass or clear coat and need machine polishing — I'll show you a test spot and quote it before going further. Afterward I seal the surface so spots don't bond as easily next time.
