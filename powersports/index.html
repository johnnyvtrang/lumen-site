Good to know

  • More than one vehicle on the same trip — at the same address, or neighbors who book together for the same visit: the highest-priced one is full price and every additional vehicle is 15% off, whether it's a car, truck, SUV, van, powersports machine, boat or RV.
  • Boats & RVs are done where they're parked — please confirm your storage lot or HOA allows washing
  • Water and power needed on-site (same as cars) · November–March: interiors, rinseless washes and lights only
  • RV roofs: I only walk on roofs rated for foot traffic
  • Busiest after Moab & dune trips (spring & fall) and before winter storage — book early

Best times to book

Before storage, and right after.

Going into storage: cleaned, decontaminated and protected, so grime, road salt, bugs and moisture don't sit on it for months, and seats, carpets and cabins dried thoroughly to help prevent mildew. Coming out: refreshed, inspected and re-protected before the season starts.

  1. 01

    Boats & jet skis

    At the end of the season, before it's covered or stored, and again in spring before the first launch.

  2. 02

    UTVs, ATVs & dirt bikes

    Before winter storage, and in spring when it comes out (plus after any Moab or dune trip).

  3. 03

    Snowmobiles

    At the end of winter, before summer storage, and at the start of winter before the first ride.

  4. 04

    Motorcycles

    In the fall before it's put away, and in spring before riding season.

  5. 05

    RVs & trailers

    Before winter storage, and in spring before the first trip.

November–March visits use interiors, rinseless washes and lights only. Prices are the same as above.
